Add Faculty

Create lecturer accounts from a spreadsheet. They sign in with Microsoft like everyone else — the email row is what links them.

How It Works

  • Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm) or CSV with a header row naming the Name and Email columns (“Faculty Name” and “Email ID” work too).
  • Each valid row becomes a lecturer account in CSE. Rows that already exist are skipped; malformed rows are reported, never silently imported.
  • New lecturers sign in with their university Microsoft account — the email is matched on first sign-in, so there is nothing to activate.
