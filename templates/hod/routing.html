How It Works

  • A request is classified from its reason: hackathons and events, curricular work, or everything else.
  • Clearing a queue sends it back to the default — the student's own proctor, then the general proctor, then the least-loaded lecturer. A queue can never be left with nowhere to go.
  • Only active lecturers can be named. If a named reviewer leaves, their queue falls through to the next default automatically.
